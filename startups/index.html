Matiks raises $10 million for brain games in the age of AI feeds

TechCrunch

Indian gaming startup Matiks raised $10 million in pre-Series A funding led by General Catalyst. It wants to steer people away from "AI-riddled timelines" with math and logic puzzles. Daily users grew from 22,000 early this year to over 300,000 by September. Arithmetic, it turns out, is the new doomscroll.
