ESG and Disclosure

EU sustainability reporting rules narrowed

squirepattonboggs.com

Directive 2026/470 narrows the scope of the Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive and the Corporate Sustainability Due Diligence Directive. Many companies will no longer be subject to these reporting obligations or will face them later. Additional requirements regarding products, packaging, and sustainability advertising will apply instead.

Read more at squirepattonboggs.com →

The Financial Conduct Authority will impose a new regulatory regime on ESG ratings providers starting in 2028. Firms must secure authorisation from the regulator to operate. The move aims to bring more reliability to the market. The full piece is for subscribers.
