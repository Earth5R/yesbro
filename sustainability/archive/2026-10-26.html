ESG and Disclosure

ISSB proposes nature-related reporting rules

ESG Today

The ISSB has released proposed nature-related corporate reporting requirements, leading a busy week in ESG news. Coca-Cola, Nestle and Danone also committed to improve packaging claims after an EU greenwashing complaint, ESG Today reports. The claims will now be improved, after the complaint.

Read more at ESG Today →

Banks seek relief from sustainable lending norms at GIFT City

The Economic Times

Banks plan to ask IFSCA for relief from rules requiring their GIFT City units to put at least 5% of the previous year's loans and investments into sustainable lending. The move follows a sharp rise in FCNR(B) deposits there, the Economic Times reports. Growth has its paperwork.

Read more at The Economic Times →

Pakistan's listed companies slip on ESG and human rights disclosure

lokmattimes.com

A Transparency International Pakistan assessment of 104 companies on the Pakistan Stock Exchange found an average transparency score of 66.73%, down from 72.3% in the previous assessment. It flagged weak disclosure on human rights, environmental, social and governance issues and anti-corruption measures.

Read more at lokmattimes.com →

India's CSR spending projected to reach 5.5 lakh crore rupees by 2030

lokmattimes.com

A CSRBOX report projects cumulative Indian CSR spending of 5.5 lakh crore rupees between April 2014 and March 2030, with annual spending rising from 10,065 crore rupees in FY 2014-15 to over 77,000 crore rupees by 2030. CSRBOX also launched a new forum, as reports tend to.

Read more at lokmattimes.com →

DAB Pumps finds 99% of its footprint comes from product use

impeller.net

DAB Pumps has published its first sustainability report, showing that about 99% of the group's carbon footprint comes from the use of its pumps after they are sold. The company says it has already beaten its 2030 target for Scope 1 and 2 emissions. The other 99% belongs to customers.

Read more at impeller.net →

Steelcase cuts operational emissions by a third since 2019

India News Network

Steelcase's 2025 Impact Report says the office furniture maker cut its Scope 1 and 2 emissions by 33% between 2019 and 2025, from 121,694 to 81,943 tonnes. That puts it about two-thirds of the way to its 2030 goal of halving operational emissions. The chairs remain comfortable.

Read more at India News Network →

DTE Energy report sets out coal exit by 2032

simplywall.st

DTE Energy's 2025 sustainability report outlines progress on retiring coal by 2032, scaling wind and solar and investing in grid reliability across Michigan. It frames decarbonisation as an infrastructure buildout tied to long-term spending and customer affordability, Simply Wall St notes. Customers will see it on the bill.

Read more at simplywall.st →

ebm-papst lifts solar capacity to 10.2 MWp

refindustry.com

The ebm-papst Group's voluntary sustainability report for 2025 to 26 shows installed solar capacity rose to 10.2 MWp. Power from its solar and gas-fired combined heat and power plants covered 14.4% of electricity use, with solar alone supplying 7.7%, Refindustry reports. Voluntary, and still published.

Read more at refindustry.com →

Telefonica updates climate plan with 2040 net zero target

Ad Hoc News

Telefonica updated its climate plan on 6 October with a net zero target for 2040. The telecom group reported a 92.8% reduction in operational emissions by 2025 against its baseline, according to AD HOC NEWS, which tracked the stock at 3.42 euros. The share price was included for balance.

ByMA presents its 2025 sustainability report

Argentina's stock exchange operator ByMA presented its 2025 sustainability report on 23 October, placing its role in the country's capital market at the centre of its investor material. The company holds a 99.96% stake in Caja de Valores, AD HOC NEWS reports. Nearly all of it, then.

Cambodian finance officials trained on IFRS sustainability standards

kiripost.com

Specialised officials and senior finance professionals in Cambodia have completed training on the IFRS S1 and S2 sustainability disclosure standards. The course, led by a UN climate advisory group member, aimed to strengthen sustainability-related financial disclosure, Kiripost reports. The acronyms have been learned.

Sustainability reporting is moving to the centre of strategic planning and capital allocation as firms face climate volatility and resource limits, say World Economic Forum governance experts. EU reaches provisional deal on carbon market reserve

londondaily.news

EU negotiators agreed on 20 October to keep the mechanism that permanently cancels some allowances held in the emissions trading system's market stability reserve, while raising the threshold at which cancellations begin. The deal is not yet law and needs endorsement from both institutions. Provisional remains the operative word.

Read more at londondaily.news →

BRAC Bank to issue Bangladesh's first bank green bond

tbsnews.net

BRAC Bank is set to issue a Tk1,000 crore green bond, becoming the first bank in Bangladesh to do so. The bond will follow Bangladesh Bank's green bond guidelines, with the launch due to be announced in Dhaka on 25 October, The Business Standard reports. Someone had to go first.

The Green Climate Fund's 46th board meeting runs in South Korea from 26 to 29 October and will consider new financing proposals, policies for its next fundraising cycle and tighter monitoring of investments. COP31 follows in Antalya from 9 to 20 November. The meetings have their own meetings.

The Philippines' Climate Change Commission will chair a new inter-agency committee on carbon markets under Executive Order 127. The order sets a governance framework for participation in international carbon markets under Article 6 of the Paris Agreement and in the voluntary market. The carbon market now has a committee.
