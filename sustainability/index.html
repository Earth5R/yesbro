EU consults on sustainability reporting standards

energylivenews.com

The EU has launched a consultation on assurance standards for sustainability reporting. Large companies must report their sustainability performance under the Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive, with information requiring independent review to be considered credible. The consultation is open to auditors, investors, and companies preparing reports.

DitchCarbon has launched an electricity module for its Scope 3 emissions platform. The feature aims to help procurement teams identify tier 1 suppliers that could reduce emissions by switching to low-carbon electricity. The company describes this as one of the fastest levers for supply chain decarbonization.
