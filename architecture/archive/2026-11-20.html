Fabric formwork reduces concrete use in beams

Building Design and Construction

MIT researchers found that software-designed concrete beams cast in fabric formwork require one-third of the concrete and half the steel of conventional beams. The design uses angled legs to remove material while maintaining load-bearing capacity. The process translates software models into flat fabric patterns.

Mass timber offers alternative to steel and concrete

kuer.org

Mass timber is gaining ground as a building method for tall structures. Layers of lumber are stacked with alternating grain directions and bonded into panels. While traditional lumber lacks the strength for skyscrapers, these engineered panels are designed to support larger structures.

Bioengineer.org

A research team has engineered high-strength structural concrete using modified limestone calcined clay cement.
