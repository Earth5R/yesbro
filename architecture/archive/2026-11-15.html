Agricultural waste repurposed for structural masonry

Bioengineer.org

Researchers Abhishek Kumar and Raju Ranjan Kumar demonstrated that agricultural combustion residues can be used in structural masonry. Replacing ten percent of fly ash with agricultural ash and natural fibres yielded compressive strengths comparable to conventional bricks. The study suggests agro-waste can maintain standard density and absorption thresholds.
