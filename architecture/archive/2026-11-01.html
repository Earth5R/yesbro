Sustainable and Green Architecture

Seoul's new buildings will supply 8 per cent of its electricity

koreatimes.co.kr

Buildings approved in Seoul between 2022 and 2026 are expected to generate 3,811 gigawatt hours of renewable energy a year, thanks to city rules requiring solar facades and geothermal heating. That is 1.5 times the annual capacity of the Seoul Thermal Power Plant. The power station has been outdesigned.

Read more at koreatimes.co.kr →

A LEED Platinum factory in Bangladesh stays 4.33 degrees cooler

Bioengineer.org

A LEED Platinum garment factory in Rangpur, run by Karupannya Rangpur Ltd., kept indoor air an average of 4.33 degrees Celsius cooler than outdoors in the warm season, a case study in City and Built Environment found. A nearby conventional building managed only 1.67 degrees.

Scientists may have solved a 66-million-year-old mystery: how crocodiles survived the asteroid that wiped out the dinosaurs.
