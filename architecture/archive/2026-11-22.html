El Niño impact on Indian monsoon rainfall

hindupost.in

Data from the Ministry of Earth Sciences indicates that 7 out of 16 El Niño years since 1950 resulted in below-normal monsoons for India. The phenomenon shifts atmospheric circulation, reducing moisture transport to the subcontinent, which relies on the Southwest Monsoon for over 70 percent of its annual rainfall.

A promising material for ultra-fast optical communications has held a secret for decades, but scientists may have finally cracked it. Researchers at KAIST and Caltech have precisely measured how barium titanate controls and absorbs light, resolving longstanding uncertainties that have slowed its development. Their findings could make it.
