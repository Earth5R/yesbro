Deccan Herald

NTA has opened applications for JEE Main 2027 Session 1 at jeemain.nta.nic.in until 22 November, with a late window from 23 to 26 November for an extra Rs 500. Admit cards are due on 13 January, and exams run on 20 to 24 and 28 January 2027.

The National Testing Agency has launched a 24x7 toll-free helpline, 1800-180-4747, now being trialled, and a help desk at 011-69227700 open from 8 am to 10 pm. It says its helplines will never ask for passwords or OTPs. Nearly 2.19 lakh candidates had registered for UGC-NET by 21 October.

Study Abroad and Visas

UK to charge universities a levy of about $1,200 per international student from 2028

africa.businessinsider.com

From August 2028, universities in England will pay an annual levy of £925 for each international student beyond an exemption threshold of 220. It covers new and existing students. Students will not pay directly, but universities may raise international fees, and leaders have criticised the plan.

Read more at africa.businessinsider.com →

UK raises the money international students must show for a student visa

independent.co.uk

The Home Office is raising the financial maintenance requirement for student visas. London students will need to show £1,570 a month, up to £14,130 for a nine-month course, while elsewhere the monthly figure rises from £1,171 to £1,203. These funds come on top of tuition and visa costs.

Read more at independent.co.uk →

Australia now requires biometrics from all visa applicants in India

streamlinefeed.co.ke

From 26 October, people lodging new Australian visa applications in India must provide fingerprints and facial images, collected through VFS Global centres. The change follows amendments to Australia's migration laws aimed at tightening identity checks and combating visa fraud, and affects students and skilled workers.

Read more at streamlinefeed.co.ke →

Some international students in Australia must now apply for new visas from overseas

The Economic Times

Students in Australia who change providers or need extra time, and do not qualify for the course completion exemption, must be outside the country both when they apply for a new student visa and when it is granted, the Department of Home Affairs says. Indian students already there are affected.

Read more at The Economic Times →

New international student and worker arrivals in Canada fall 63%

immigcanada.com

Canada recorded 195,335 new international student and temporary worker arrivals from January to August 2026, including 65,240 students. That is 326,900 fewer, or 63% lower, than the same period of 2024. The government aims to bring temporary residents below five percent of the population.

Read more at immigcanada.com →
