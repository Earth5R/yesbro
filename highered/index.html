New Zealand cuts research master's students' work rights

The Economic Times

New Zealand made four student visa changes effective immediately. Master's-by-research students lose unlimited in-study work rights, English ability becomes an explicit visa factor, and INZ can suspend processing in more circumstances. The fourth change gives students more time after their course to decide their next step.

Read more at The Economic Times →

Australia slips as students' first-choice destination, survey finds

Times Higher Education

A survey of over 4,800 mostly prospective students found visa uncertainty is pushing interest away from Australia. Twenty-four per cent named it their preferred destination, down from 31% six months earlier. The UK and US were each top choice for 21%, up from 19% and 14%.

macrobusiness.com.au

Australia has raised the cost, paperwork and scrutiny of student visas to deter non-genuine students. The English requirement rose from IELTS 5.5 to 6.0 in March 2024, and the visa fee more than doubled from $710 to $1,600 in July 2024. Repeated onshore 'visa hopping' has also been restricted.

OECD data shows growing competition for graduate-level international students

New OECD indicators show international mobility rising at master's and doctoral level, with especially high foreign shares in Luxembourg, New Zealand and Switzerland. International students are substantially more likely than domestic ones to graduate in STEM, and even stricter countries are making exceptions for advanced-degree applicants.

About 5% of the world's 7.3 million international students study in Germany, roughly five times its share of world population, The PIE News reports. Five years ago the debate centred on the English-speaking big four. Germany now sits in what ICEF calls the big 14, with Ireland, France and Malaysia.
