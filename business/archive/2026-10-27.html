How wealthy families use trusts to pass on wealth

Mint

Family trusts hold assets for relatives under the Indian Trusts Act, 1882. A revocable trust lets the settlor undo it, but its income is taxed as the settlor's, so it saves no tax. An irrevocable trust gives up control for stronger protection. A trust is no automatic tax shield.
