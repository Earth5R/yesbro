Fooling Yourself: how narratives shape beliefs

arXiv · 2026-11-19

Announce Type: replace Abstract: Narratives often combine diagnostic evidence with truthful but nondiagnostic details. In a two-state experiment with known signal likelihoods, we test whether such details distort belief updating. Although Bayes' rule prescribes no revision, nondiagnostic signals induce revisions. Preprint, not peer reviewed.
