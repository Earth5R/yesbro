Stronger flu shots linked to lower Alzheimer's risk

ScienceDaily

Researchers at UTHealth Houston found that adults aged 65 and older who received a high-dose flu vaccine had a 55% lower risk of developing Alzheimer's disease. Standard-dose vaccines were associated with a 40% reduction. The study analyzed data from nearly 200,000 patients.

Read more at ScienceDaily →

Alkaline phosphatase trial fails to prevent kidney injury

Bioengineer.org

A phase 2 trial of the drug ilofotase alfa found it did not significantly reduce acute kidney injury in 204 high-risk patients undergoing cardiac surgery. The condition complicates up to 30 percent of on-pump procedures and currently lacks any approved pharmacological prevention methods.

A Mayo Clinic autopsy study found women with Parkinson's disease are more than twice as likely as men to carry a high burden of Alzheimer's-associated amyloid plaques. The findings suggest a need for sex-specific strategies for managing Parkinson's dementia, a condition that currently lacks disease-modifying therapies.

consultqd.clevelandclinic.org

Reducing elevated levels of lipoprotein(a) with the drug pelacarsen had no effect on cardiovascular events in patients with pre-existing heart disease, according to the Lp(a)HORIZON trial. Investigators noted the results were unexpected, though they maintained it is not the end of the story for the therapy.

Pelacarsen trial misses primary cardiovascular endpoint

cleveland.com

The Phase 3 Lp(a)HORIZON trial found that while pelacarsen reduced lipoprotein(a) levels by 78.4% after one year, it did not reduce major cardiovascular events. Patients taking the drug experienced heart attacks and strokes at nearly the same rate as those receiving a placebo.
