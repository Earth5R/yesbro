Medicine and Research

First human trial of a B7-H4 drug conjugate shows tumour responses

Bioengineer.org

The Phase 1/2a BLUESTAR study of AZD8205, or puxitatug samrotecan, reported partial tumour responses in previously treated patients with advanced ovarian, endometrial and breast cancer. It enrolled 47 patients with B7-H4-positive solid tumours, and results were published in Nature Communications.

Read more at Bioengineer.org →

Hospital patients treated by female physicians fared better, Ontario study finds

temertymedicine.utoronto.ca

Patients treated in hospital by female physicians were less likely to die or be readmitted than those treated by male physicians, according to a University of Toronto and ICES study in JAMA Network Open. The authors describe it as an association among patients with common medical diagnoses.

Read more at temertymedicine.utoronto.ca →

Retatrutide reduced sleep apnoea symptoms in a trial

medicalnewstoday.com

A study in people with overweight or obesity found that the experimental weight-loss drug retatrutide greatly reduced obstructive sleep apnoea symptoms, Medical News Today reports. The condition pauses breathing during sleep and is often undiagnosed, so there are no definitive figures on how common it is.

Read more at medicalnewstoday.com →

Untrained dogs may detect a stranger's seizure by scent

ScienceAlert

University of Milan researchers presented sweat samples from a six-year-old girl with epilepsy, taken during and outside seizures, to 30 dogs unfamiliar with her, ScienceAlert reports. The study suggests even untrained dogs with no detection experience may pick out seizure samples.

Read more at ScienceAlert →

FDA approves NRx's preservative-free ketamine injection

TradingView

The US FDA has approved NRx Pharmaceuticals' abbreviated new drug application for preservative-free ketamine injection, its first approved product, ahead of a 10 November goal date. CEO Jonathan Javitt said approval came in under 14 months, against an average of 35.6 months cited from FDA data.

Read more at TradingView →

courant.com

Dr Thuy Tran, Yale site principal investigator for the Phase 2 and 3 trials of Moderna and Merck's personalised melanoma vaccine, told the Hartford Courant it is 'the first cancer vaccine that's proven efficacy in advanced Phase 3 clinical trials'. Melanoma kills more than 8,000 Americans a year.

A swab test may spare some women an endometrial biopsy

Medical Xpress

Researchers report that a simple vaginal swab may one day help women with abnormal bleeding learn whether they have endometrial cancer without first going through a painful biopsy, Medical Xpress reports. The full article was not accessible.

Abemaciclib can raise creatinine without kidney damage, study finds

Medical Xpress

Researchers from Kanazawa University and Shinshu University found that the breast cancer drug abemaciclib often raises creatinine in a way that makes kidneys look like they are failing when they are not, Medical Xpress reports. A baseline measurement can help doctors tell the difference.

Read more at Medical Xpress →

University of Chicago team targets radiation's immune side effect

Medical Xpress

Researchers at the University of Chicago have identified a mechanism to make immune cells reprogrammed by radiation therapy attack tumours rather than protect them, in a study in Cancer Cell, Medical Xpress reports. Radiation activates a protein called TET2 on immune cells in bone marrow.

Read more at Medical Xpress →

A ten-marker blood score aims to track healthy ageing

Medical Xpress

European researchers have created a personalised indicator using ten blood markers to flag disease risk and assess healthy ageing, Medical Xpress reports. Studies suggest about 60% of people over 65 live with two or more chronic conditions, which the work hopes to address.

Read more at Medical Xpress →

Hepatitis E vaccine well tolerated in women of childbearing age

Bioengineer.org

An observational study in Karachi found the hepatitis E vaccine Hecolin safe and well tolerated among 375 non-pregnant women of childbearing age, with only mild, transient side effects over 21 days of follow-up. It was published in the Egyptian Liver Journal.

India bars imports of drugs with under a year of shelf life

Medical Dialogues

The Health Ministry has amended Rule 31 of the Drugs Rules, 1945, to bar imports of drugs with less than one year of residual shelf life on the import date, Medical Dialogues reports. Biologicals and radiopharmaceuticals may be imported with 60% of their labelled shelf life left.

India's Patent Office has rejected a TB Alliance patent application covering the BPaL regimen of bedaquiline, pretomanid and linezolid for drug-resistant tuberculosis, in a decision issued on 27 October. It followed pre-grant oppositions filed in July 2020. Access advocates see it as a win for affordable medicines.
