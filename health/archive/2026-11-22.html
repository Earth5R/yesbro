Nasal mRNA treatment improves memory in aged mice

Study Finds

Weekly nasal drops carrying mRNA for the protein BDNF matched the memory scores of 18-month-old mice to 3-month-old animals. The treatment, which also showed efficacy in mice with Alzheimer-type damage, successfully delivered protein to the hippocampus without spreading to other organs. Mice are currently enjoying the results.

Researchers at Harvard Medical School and the University of California San Diego have accelerated imaging techniques to map large volumes of brain tissue in nanoscale detail. The approach reveals fine structures that traditional connectomes can miss. The brain is finally being asked to show its work.

Daraxonrasib combo shows promise in pancreatic cancer trial

geo.tv

A phase 1/2 trial presented in Barcelona reports that the oral medication daraxonrasib, combined with chemotherapy, caused tumour shrinkage in pancreatic cancer patients. In one in 12 patients, the cancer became undetectable. The treatment is being called the biggest breakthrough for the disease in decades.

Real-world study confirms dupilumab reduces chronic prurigo nodularis itch

Bioengineer.org

A retrospective study in Dermatology and Therapy shows that dupilumab provides durable clinical improvement for adults with chronic prurigo nodularis. By inhibiting type 2 neuro-immune signaling, the drug cleared severe skin lesions. The findings confirm efficacy previously established in randomized clinical trials, now occurring in routine practice.
