Medicine and Research

A pancreatic cancer vaccine drew immune responses in nearly every patient

ScienceAlert

In a randomised phase 2 trial led by a Mayo Clinic researcher, nearly every patient who got the vaccine developed an immune response against mutated KRAS, found in about 90 percent of pancreatic ductal adenocarcinomas. ScienceAlert notes this does not prove it stops the cancer returning.

Read more at ScienceAlert →

Japan treats its first patient with a stem cell therapy for Parkinson's

Medical Xpress

A patient has undergone a stem cell therapy for Parkinson's disease, the Japanese drugmaker behind it said on Friday, a world first following the treatment's conditional approval in Japan, Medical Xpress reports. The treatment's long term results are yet to be reported.

Read more at Medical Xpress →

High dose omega 3 may lengthen survival in some advanced colon cancer

healthday.com

A trial found some patients with advanced colon cancer who took high daily doses of the omega 3 fat EPA had higher five year overall survival, HealthDay reports. The cancer was not stopped completely, and the finding applies only to some patients.

Read more at healthday.com →

FDA drops black box warnings on three more menopause patches without advisers

The Guardian

Three more menopause patches will no longer carry black box warnings about cardiovascular disease, breast cancer and dementia, the FDA said on Friday. It did not convene an advisory committee and offered few details on the science, the Guardian reports. Six treatments lost warnings earlier.

Read more at The Guardian →

A tumour atlas looks at why colorectal cancer hits young patients harder

STAT

Dana-Farber's Kimmie Ng published a Nature Medicine study mapping colorectal tumours to find biological differences in early onset cases, STAT reports. Patients under 35 may fare worse than people in their 40s or 60s despite more aggressive treatment and better baseline health.

Read more at STAT →
More from Medicine and Research

Fish oil did not protect thinking in people at risk of Alzheimer's

healthline.com

A two year trial of high dose omega 3 in older adults at raised risk of Alzheimer's found no improvement in memory, thinking or brain structure, Healthline reports. Omega 3 levels rose in the blood and brain fluid. The test scores did not follow.

Read more at healthline.com →

Albumin does not prevent kidney injury in intensive care, review finds

Bioengineer.org

A meta-analysis of nine randomised trials with 11,778 patients, published in Critical Care, found human serum albumin, widely used for fluid resuscitation in intensive care, did not significantly reduce acute kidney injury in critically ill patients.

Read more at Bioengineer.org →

Patients treated by female doctors in hospital may do better

Medical Xpress

An ICES study led by a University of Toronto researcher found patients cared for by female physicians in hospital were less likely to die or be readmitted than those treated by male physicians, Medical Xpress reports. The researchers describe an association.

Read more at Medical Xpress →

Review finds no evidence testosterone helps menopause brain fog

Medical Xpress

A UCL study says there is not enough evidence to determine whether testosterone helps thinking and memory in midlife women, even though it is often prescribed in the UK for that reason, Medical Xpress reports.

Read more at Medical Xpress →

Prednisolone gave long Covid patients a modest, short lived lift

Medical Xpress

A treatment trial of the steroid prednisolone for post Covid syndrome showed a modest and short lived improvement, Medical Xpress reports. Reliable data on which treatments work for persistent fatigue, breathlessness and concentration problems remain scarce.

Read more at Medical Xpress →

Keralam study finds distinct gut bacteria in Parkinson's patients

The Hindu

Researchers from CUSAT and SCTIMST in Thiruvananthapuram found significant differences between the gut bacteria of Parkinson's patients and healthy people, in a study published in npj Parkinson's Disease, The Hindu reports. Scientists have long suspected the gut and brain talk constantly.

Read more at The Hindu →

Air pollution aged bone marrow stem cells faster in diabetic mice

The Indian Express

A mouse study found fine particulate matter can accelerate ageing in bone marrow stem cells and weaken their ability to regenerate, with stronger effects in mice with type 2 diabetes, the Indian Express reports. The findings so far come from mice.

Read more at The Indian Express →

Blocking one molecular interaction reduced heart scarring in mice

Medical Xpress

Researchers at Baylor College of Medicine and the University of Oxford found blocking a specific molecular interaction reduced atrial fibrosis and abnormal rhythms in mouse models and in cells from patients with atrial fibrillation, Medical Xpress reports. It is early stage work.

Read more at Medical Xpress →
