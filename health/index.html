Narrower surgical margins may work for a childhood sarcoma

health.ucdavis.edu

A UC Davis led trial, the first large prospective study of its kind, suggests that in paediatric non rhabdomyosarcoma soft tissue sarcomas, cancer control may be achieved with much narrower margins than thought, sparing nerves, vessels and joints.

Lilly's retatrutide beats Ozempic's top dose on weight loss

nypost.com

In a head to head trial, Eli Lilly's experimental shot retatrutide helped patients lose more than twice as much weight as Ozempic's highest approved dose and did better at lowering blood sugar, the New York Post reports. It is not yet approved.

Lower doses of IPF drugs may not raise progression risk

pulmonaryfibrosisnews.com

A real world study of more than 1,500 people with idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis found that reducing doses of Ofev or Esbriet when side effects were hard to tolerate was not linked to a significantly higher risk of progression.

Covid and flu shots together were well tolerated in pregnancy

drugtopics.com

A survey study in Vaccine found Covid 19 vaccination during pregnancy was safe and well tolerated when given alongside flu shots. The most common reactions were injection site pain, redness, fatigue and muscle aches, and nobody needed medical attention.

Your blood pressure could be dangerous even when your numbers look normal

ScienceDaily · 2026-10-29

Researchers have discovered a small group of brain cells that prevents dangerous blood pressure swings, even when average readings appear normal. The finding reveals a hidden source of cardiovascular risk and could open the door to new treatments that protect the heart and brain.

ScienceDaily · 2026-10-25

Missing blood pressure medication could mean losing much of its protection against heart attacks and strokes, according to an analysis of more than 91,000 people. Patients who consistently took their medication experienced an 11% reduction in major cardiovascular events, while those who missed too many doses saw little.
