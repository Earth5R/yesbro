Wolbachia-carrying mosquitoes reduce dengue and chikungunya in Brazil

The Hindu

A study of 5,700 children found a 69 percent reduction in dengue and chikungunya infections in neighbourhoods populated by mosquitoes bred to carry Wolbachia bacteria. The findings suggest a potential alternative to relying solely on pesticides to control the spread of these viruses.

New research published in Cancer suggests that increasing and persistently high viral loads in women with HPV infections may indicate the presence of cervical cancer. The study highlights the potential diagnostic value of monitoring viral trends in patients with persistent infections.

Bioengineer.org

A phase 1 clinical trial at City of Hope evaluated CD123-directed CAR T-cell therapy in patients with relapsed or refractory acute myeloid leukemia. Researchers reported a manageable safety profile with no dose-limiting toxicities, even among patients who had previously undergone allogeneic hematopoietic cell transplantation.

Erasca reported that its experimental pill, ERAS-0015, reduced tumours in 44 percent of pancreatic cancer patients and 69 percent of those with specific genetic variations in an early-stage trial. India reports decline in tuberculosis burden

factly.in

India's TB incidence rate fell from 237 cases per lakh in 2015 to 187 in 2024, with mortality dropping from 28 to 21 deaths per lakh. Despite these improvements, the figures remain above national elimination targets. Testing volumes have increased to 2.8 crore in 2025.
