Azacitidine mechanism re-evaluated

Medical Xpress

Azacitidine has been a standard treatment for blood cancers for over two decades. While long thought to function by modifying DNA to reactivate silenced genes, new research suggests the drug acts primarily on RNA. The drug has been working, just not in the way previously assumed.

Daraxonrasib shows promise in pancreatic cancer trial

Medical Xpress

A combination of daraxonrasib and chemotherapy resulted in substantial tumor shrinkage for patients with metastatic pancreatic cancer. These findings come from a Phase I/II clinical trial. The treatment is intended for cancer that has already spread to other parts of the body.

University of Liverpool scientists have identified the specific positioning of a protein within nerve cells associated with a severe form of childhood epilepsy. Medical Xpress

Intrathoracic adhesions often complicate repeat surgeries, yet their molecular origins remain poorly understood. A Japanese research team has developed an animal model to replicate these pleural adhesions. They aim to identify the specific cells and signaling pathways responsible for the formation of the internal scar tissue.

Medical Xpress

A Monash University study has mapped the genetic changes that cause immune cells to lose effectiveness with age.
