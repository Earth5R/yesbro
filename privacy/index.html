My Paper sign-in

Yes Bro offers optional Google sign-in for My Paper. When you sign in, we store your name, email, profile photo and the topics you chose, so your paper can follow you across devices.

We use that information to run My Paper and to understand reader interests in aggregate. It is not sold.

Trackers

Yes Bro does not add ad trackers or separate analytics products on top of the site. Pages are static files. Sign-in uses Google only when you choose it.

Deletion

To ask us to delete your My Paper account data, email ontheworldroad@gmail.com.
