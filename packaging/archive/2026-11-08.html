JK Paper Limited has completed a restructuring scheme involving the amalgamation of three subsidiaries and demerger operations. The company allotted 1.19 crore equity shares to shareholders of Enviro Tech Ventures Limited. This move increases the total paid up capital of the firm to 181.32 crore.
