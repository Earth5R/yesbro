Plenty of Indian students look up at a plane and think, I could do that. In 2026 the industry seems to agree. Here is what it actually takes, using this week's news and the programme pages themselves.

## Demand is real, and so is the training boom

By 30 June 2026, India had issued 1,331 Commercial Pilot Licences, and 540 of them, or 41%, went to cadets trained outside India, [Aviation Today reported](https://aviationtoday.in/indian-aviation/indias-pilot-training-boom-faces-test-of-safety-standards-and-industry-maturity/). The same piece makes a sharp point. India is producing pilots at a pace it has never seen, but growth is not the same as maturity, and the quality of training matters as much as the speed.

Airlines are signing cadets in batches. Skyborne celebrated 48 cadets who signed letters of intent for IndiGo's Cadet Pilot Programme, its largest intake, [AFM reported](https://afm.aero/skyborne-marks-new-milestone-with-largest-intake-of-indigo-cadets). They start at a ground school in Delhi, spend 12 months flight training in Vero Beach, Florida, then return for licence conversion and type rating before joining IndiGo as A320 junior first officers. Fleets are growing too. Akasa Air now has 44 aircraft and expects its first Boeing 737 MAX 10 in 2027, [Hotelier Buzz reported](https://hotelierbuzz.com/akasa-air-fleet-44-boeing-737-max-10/).

## The cadet route, step by step

Cadet programmes bundle training with a conditional job offer. Take the IndiGo programme run with Garuda Aviation Academy as an example. Its [registration page](https://garudaaviation.in/registration) lists:

- Age 18 to 40, Indian citizen or OCI
- 10+2 with at least 51% in each of English, Maths and Physics, with NIOS accepted if you did not take those subjects
- A DGCA Class 2 medical when you apply and a Class 1 medical before signing the letter of intent
- 21 months of training across Delhi NCR and South Africa
- A course fee of $131,000, with bank loans of up to 90% offered subject to eligibility

Selection runs through the ADAPT aptitude test, then a group activity and an interview with IndiGo. The page lists a registration deadline of 5 November for the next batch, with GAPI from 1 to 4 December. If you are thinking about it, that date is close.

## Read the cost line twice

That fee is the part most people skim. A conditional job offer reduces risk. It does not remove it, because you still have to clear every exam and check ride. The Garuda page notes DGCA exams must be cleared within 12 months of starting ground school. Before you sign a loan, ask what happens if you fail a stage, how many attempts you get and when repayment starts.

## The rules about rest

Pilot life is also about fatigue. The DGCA has extended full implementation of its revised Flight Duty Time Limitations by five months to 31 March 2027, the third extension, [Free Press Journal reported](https://www.freepressjournal.in/india/dgca-extends-pilot-rest-rule-deadline-to-march-2027-airlines-get-five-month-relief). The Federation of Indian Pilots opposed the delay, telling the civil aviation minister that commercial or operational convenience cannot take precedence over safety, [The Telegraph reported](https://www.telegraphindia.com/amp/business/commercial-convenience-cant-override-safety-pilots-body-flags-proposed-relaxation-of-flight-duty-norms/cid/2185927). Future pilots should follow this closely, because these rules will shape your rosters, your nights and your health.

## Looking abroad

Global airlines are recruiting cadets too, though many restrict eligibility by nationality or residency. Today's Yes Bro Aviation list includes [Cathay Pacific's cadet programme](https://flygoshjobs.com/job/Cadet%20Pilot%20Programme/4ADBC7792162B805EFEF468C553D4886), open to people with the right to live and work in Hong Kong or mainland China and closing on 11 November, and [Wizz Air's She Can Fly academy](https://skystudyatpl.com/pilot-jobs/28421a8a-923f-418a-8f4c-97454363f07c-wizz-air-pilot-academy-she-can-fly-programme), listed with a 24 November deadline. Always check the airline's own page before applying.

## Not only pilots

Aviation careers do not all sit in the cockpit. Today's jobs list also includes engineering roles at Airbus, Boeing and GE Aerospace in Bengaluru and Pune. And for younger students, the military route has its moments. Air Chief Marshal AP Singh flew a sortie with NCC cadet Jakkula Tejasvi Reddy at the Air Force Academy in Dundigal, [defence.in reported](https://defence.in/threads/air-chief-marshal-ap-singh-pilots-kiran-aircraft-with-ncc-cadet-during-exclusive-dundigal-training-sortie.18939/).

## The short version

Check the eligibility list today, especially the medical, because a Class 1 medical can end a dream before any money is spent. Compare programmes on total cost, training location and failure policy, not just the airline logo. Treat deadlines as real. And remember that the job is about judgement and rest as much as flying. The sky is busy. Arrive prepared.
