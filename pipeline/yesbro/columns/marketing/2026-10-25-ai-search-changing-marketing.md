For twenty years marketing had a simple rule. Rank on Google and people will find you. In 2026 the first stop might be a chatbot, a video that answers instead of plays, or an assistant that already has an opinion. The rule has not died, but it now has fine print. This week's research shows what changed and, usefully, what did not.

## Discovery moved. Decisions did not

A Feedvisor survey found 16% of shoppers now start looking for products on ChatGPT, but only 9% go there to buy, and only 5% buy on the spot when an AI assistant recommends something, [Modern Retail reported via Digiday](https://digiday.com/marketing/ai-boosts-discovery-but-reviews-still-seal-the-deal-new-research-shows/). The rest check Google and Amazon first. PartnerCentric found that after an AI suggestion, 33% of shoppers go and check reviews.

So AI is the new shop window, and your reviews, prices and product pages are still the till. If those are thin, the chatbot's recommendation sends people elsewhere.

## The Google gap nobody is minding

Small businesses may be looking the wrong way. The BrightLocal Small Business Marketing Report 2026 surveyed over 1,000 US small business owners and found 84% are confident customers can find them online, [MarTech Cube reported](https://www.martechcube.com/brightlocal-report-finds-small-businesses-losing-google-visibility-to-ai/). Yet 71% of consumers use Google during local searches, while only 22% of owners rank Google among their top two acquisition channels. Word of mouth at 35% and social media at 33% rank higher.

That is a confidence gap with a cost. Before you chase AI visibility, make sure your basic listings, hours and reviews are right.

## Answers are replacing clicks

YouTube is widening its Ask YouTube trial, an AI tool that turns video content into direct text answers with timestamped clips, to more US users across desktop, mobile and TV, [Vietnam.vn reported](https://www.vietnam.vn/en/youtube-thu-tim-kiem-bang-ai-nha-sang-tao-lo-mat-view) citing Search Engine Land. Creators worry about lost views. For brands, the lesson is that content needs to be the clearest answer, not just the most clicked video. YouTube's chief executive Neal Mohan told [Forbes India](https://www.forbesindia.com/article/global-game/cross-border/youtubes-neal-mohan-on-taming-ai-content-while-backing-human-creators/2999805/1) about backing human creators while managing AI content.

## Let the machine help, but check its work

Google added brand management tools to Gemini, including brand content creation and audience insights, with links to Adobe, Canva and Shopify, [Social Media Today reported](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/google-adds-brand-management-tools-to-gemini/833790/). Handy. But automation has a sting in its tail. Bookshop.org and the agency Exverus say Meta's automated tools changed their ads without approval, and some advertisers are pulling campaigns as Meta's new terms from 30 October shift responsibility for AI output onto businesses, [ContentGrip reported](https://www.contentgrip.com/meta-ai-ad-edits-liability/). If the AI writes it and you run it, it is your ad.

ContentGrip also has sensible advice for budget season. A feature announcement is [not proof it works](https://www.contentgrip.com/martech-feature-readiness/) for your team, so check regional availability and integrations before you sign.

## Speed still sells

A Gladly survey of 1,022 US online shoppers found 46% left a site ready to buy because a question went unanswered, and 53% gave up while waiting for a reply, [MarTech Cube reported](https://www.martechcube.com/46-of-frequent-shoppers-have-left-a-site-ready-to-buy-gladly-survey/). The most advanced funnel in the world loses to a slow chat window.

## Meanwhile in India

On television, celebrity endorsed ad volumes fell 17% from January to September, though celebrities still appeared in 25% of all TV commercials, according to TAM AdEx data reported by [afaqs](https://www.afaqs.com/news/mktg/shah-rukh-khan-leads-tv-ad-visibility-as-celebrity-ad-volumes-fall-17-12742003). Shah Rukh Khan led with 26 hours of daily ad visibility. And the jobs reflect the new mix. Today's Yes Bro Marketing list includes a [Finance Content Creator](https://job-boards.eu.greenhouse.io/groww/jobs/5000706101) role at Groww and an early career [brand and communication role](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/sarvam/bba82f34-1544-421e-a393-3edd8a5f4cb2) at Sarvam AI.

## A five point checklist

Fix your Google listings and reviews first. Write pages that answer real questions clearly. Reply to customers fast. Read every AI generated ad before it runs. And measure sales, not impressions. Marketers who want to learn what changes behaviour, not just clicks, can try Earth5R's [FIELDWORK fellowship](https://earth5r.org/fieldwork/), which means real conversations with real people on the ground. The search box has changed. The customer, it turns out, still wants proof.
