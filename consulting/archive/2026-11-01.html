Capgemini reported third-quarter revenue of 6.07 billion euros, up 11.7 per cent at constant currency, helped by the WNS and Cloud4C acquisitions and AI-led projects. It raised its full-year growth target to 9 to 9.5 per cent, from about 8.5 to 9. It is the second upgrade this year.
