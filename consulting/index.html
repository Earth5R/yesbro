EY UK partners take home £870,000 as consulting shrinks again

Big4 News

EY's UK partners received average distributable profits of £870,000 for the year to 3 July 2026, up about 10.5%, while UK fee income rose 2% to £3.86 billion, Big4News reports. Consulting revenue fell 3% for a third straight year. EY-Parthenon grew 6%.

news.bloombergtax.com

PwC reported nearly flat revenue growth of 0.7% in local currency across Europe, the Middle East and Africa, against more than 3% at Deloitte and EY, Bloomberg Tax reports. A year-long block on Saudi wealth fund advisory contracts weighed on the region, industry watchers say.

Accenture shares slip 4.41% after a multi-week rally

Accenture stock fell 4.41% on October 27 while its sector rose 0.41%, TradingKey reports, attributing the move to profit-taking after an aggressive rally. The shares had climbed to recent highs on strong fourth-quarter results, record client bookings and enterprise AI partnerships. Gravity remains undefeated.

McKinsey says agentic AI could cut bank KYC time by up to 70%

Devdiscourse

Agentic AI could reduce the time banks spend on know-your-customer processes by 30 to 70 per cent, according to a McKinsey report covered by Devdiscourse. Banks often assign 10 to 15 percent of staff to KYC and anti-money laundering work, but oversight costs and governance gaps slow adoption.

Read more at Devdiscourse →

BCG expects loan growth to shield Indian bank profits in FY27

kalingatv.com

Robust loan demand, widening margins and steady asset quality should support Indian bank earnings through FY27, a Boston Consulting Group assessment says, with loans seen rising 18%, Kalinga TV reports. West Asia, which accounts for 14 to 20 percent of India's trade, is the main external risk.

Read more at kalingatv.com →

WWD and BCG map fashion growth for a more careful shopper

wwd.com

A WWD x BCG Future of Fashion Report, released at the WWD Apparel and Retail CEO Summit, finds shoppers more wary and price-conscious and researching purchases more carefully. It draws on BCG surveys of more than 4,000 consumers and nine million social media posts. Impulse buying is under review.

Read more at wwd.com →

WGC and BCG see digital gold reaching up to $170 billion by 2031

indianjeweller.in

A World Gold Council and BCG report projects the digital gold market could grow from an estimated $45 to 55 billion to $160 to 170 billion by 2031, about 27% a year in its base case. It is based on a survey of more than 12,000 retail investors.

Read more at indianjeweller.in →

KPMG says 36% of Indians could not afford a healthy diet in 2025

Business Standard

About 520.1 million people in India, or 35.5% of the population, could not afford a healthy diet in 2025, a KPMG in India report says, Business Standard reports. Per capita food consumption remains about 20% below requirements even though production is around 36% above them.

Read more at Business Standard →

Deutsche Bank expects the Reserve Bank of India to hike by 25 basis points in December 2026 and another 50 in the first half of 2027, taking the repo rate to 6.25%, The Economic Times reports. The forecast, more hawkish than consensus, assumes growth above 7% with elevated inflation.

Banking system surplus liquidity fell to Rs 50,000 crore as of October 23, largely on GST outflows and higher cash reserve requirements, an ICICI Bank report says. The RBI has started variable-rate repo auctions, including a seven-day Rs 1 lakh crore operation, The Economic Times reports.
