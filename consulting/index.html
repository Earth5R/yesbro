McKinsey's Joydeep Sengupta says founder CEOs fumble succession

Business Standard

Founder CEOs need to pay more attention to succession if their companies are to outlast them, McKinsey senior partner Joydeep Sengupta told a Business Standard summit. "Our research shows that two-thirds of them don't manage the succession very well," he said. Some look for successors in their own image.

Former Deloitte UK head Richard Houston received £7.1 million last year, up 45%, while the firm cut 1,000 jobs and reduced profit shares for new partners. It included a £1.3 million one-off award, filings show. He is now the best-paid UK boss in the Big Four.

"Nearly 1 in 2 consumers are actively considering an E2W for their next two-wheeler purchase," a BCG survey of 1,600 owners and buyers found, with outright rejection down from 38% to 29%. Electric models make up 9.9% of two-wheeler sales so far in FY27, up from 5.5% in FY24.
