The Big Firms

Accenture says AI token use may jump 78% as prices fall

ANI News

An Accenture Research report expects average AI token prices to fall about 19% over the next two years while token consumption rises about 78%, so overall AI spend may not drop. Only 35% of organisations can precisely calculate the cost per business outcome of their busiest AI use case.

Read more at ANI News →

Murata ties part of Accenture's fees to results in an AI deal

BigGo Finance

Murata Manufacturing has signed a three-year partnership with Accenture to transform its factories with AI and data, covering quality control, digital verification, IT infrastructure and talent. For some initiatives, part of Accenture's pay will be tied to shared performance targets. The invoice now has a scorecard attached.

Read more at BigGo Finance →

Accenture CEO Julie Sweet buys shares worth over $3 million

TradingView

Accenture chair and CEO Julie Sweet bought 12,200 Class A shares in two transactions at weighted average prices of about $245 and $246, a total of $3,001,041, according to a Form 4 filing. She now directly owns 48,910 shares, TradingView reports.

Read more at TradingView →

Eisner Advisory CEO says AI will end billing by the hour

The Economic Times

Eisner Advisory CEO Charles Weinstein told The Economic Times that private equity changed the ownership model of accounting firms and AI will change the business model. EisnerAmper, in India since 2006, now has about 1,100 people there. The billable hour has been told to start updating its CV.

Read more at The Economic Times →

Deloitte Anjin and two Korean law firms sign an AI compliance pact

Deloitte Anjin Accounting and law firms Daeryook & Aju and Lin signed an MOU in Seoul on 28 October to help companies with AI compliance, combining legal advice with AI risk management consulting. The pact responds to Korea's Basic AI Act and the EU's AI Act.

McKinsey finds AI tools now steer the start of shopping

retailtechinnovationhub.com

A McKinsey survey of about 21,000 consumers in 11 markets found 29% of AI shoppers say AI chat or search tools most influenced their latest purchase, triple the share citing search engines. US AI users completing purchases through AI rose from 10% to 17% since December 2025.

Read more at retailtechinnovationhub.com →

WEF and BCG say digital asset rules now reach beyond crypto

Crypto Briefing

A World Economic Forum and BCG report covering 35 jurisdictions argues that tokenisation and digital money may matter more than Bitcoin for how rules are written. Titled Regulation of Digital Assets in a Shifting Global Landscape, it says digital assets are reshaping how money moves and markets work.

Read more at Crypto Briefing →

KPMG's India CEO Outlook finds bosses optimistic despite risks

roastbrief.us

KPMG's 2026 India CEO Outlook, based on responses from 125 CEOs in India, finds a leadership group that remains optimistic despite cyber threats, regulatory change and rapid AI adoption. CEOs are investing in cyber resilience, AI governance, workforce readiness and sustainability, the report says.

Read more at roastbrief.us →

Bain sees Europe's construction recovering slowly to 2029

Bain and Company

Bain & Company's Building Blocks Construction Indicator forecasts gradual recovery in European construction through 2029, with 2026 better than last year. New housing is recovering more slowly than expected, held back by higher interest rates, stretched affordability and cost pressures, so a full rebound is unlikely before 2029.

Read more at Bain and Company →

BCG survey finds 87% satisfaction with Qatar's digital services

BCG's sixth biennial Digital Government Citizen Survey, run across 44 countries, found 87% of respondents in Qatar satisfied with digital government services, with usage of those services at 40%. The report says AI is now reshaping what citizens across the GCC expect from them.

Cabinet approves a ₹10,000 crore container manufacturing scheme

The Economic Times

The Union Cabinet has approved a ₹10,000 crore Container Manufacturing Scheme that will run for 13 years and aims to raise annual domestic capacity nearly tenfold to 750,000 TEUs by 2040. It also approved a ₹1,856.67 crore scheme for sports goods manufacturing.

Read more at The Economic Times →

Textiles PLI scheme extended to 2034 with easier norms

The Economic Times

The government has extended the Production Linked Incentive scheme for textiles until 2034 and cut the minimum turnover requirement for some manufacturers from ₹200 crore to ₹100 crore to draw in smaller firms. A reopened portal will accept applications from 1 November to 31 December.

Read more at The Economic Times →

Nasscom seeks more time on GST for ecommerce delivery

Business Standard

Nasscom has asked the Finance Ministry to defer revised GST rules for delivery services through ecommerce operators until 1 January 2027, citing technology, accounting and compliance challenges. The change was recommended by the 57th GST Council, and Nasscom filed its representation on 28 October.

Read more at Business Standard →

Online sellers can use ecommerce warehouses as a GST base

The Economic Times

From 1 November, small online sellers can expand into states where they have no premises by using an ecommerce platform's warehouse as their local base, under a new GST registration route. CBIC also revised rules on scrap purchases and exporter refunds and expanded e-invoicing from January.

Read more at The Economic Times →

CBIC says GST officers must prove intent before invoking fraud provisions

The Economic Times

The CBIC has said Sections 74(1) and 74A(5)(ii) of the CGST Act can be invoked only where there is specific evidence of intent to evade tax, such as fraud or wilful misstatement. Officers cannot apply them automatically just because scrutiny finds a discrepancy.

Read more at The Economic Times →

The Economic Times

Sebi chair Pandey said some compliance requirements for foreign portfolio investors in India are more onerous than in other major jurisdictions, and the regulator will issue a consultation paper on aligning them with global standards while keeping investor protection safeguards. RBI deputy governor says India can cut its debt ratio by 2031

The Economic Times

RBI Deputy Governor Poonam Gupta said India is projected to be the only emerging economy among nine with GDP above $1 trillion to reduce its government debt-to-GDP ratio between 2025 and 2031. General government debt is seen falling from 84.3% to 75.1% of GDP.

BusinessLine

India will seek further discussions with the UK on its Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism, due from 1 January 2027, BusinessLine reports. London has recognised India's Carbon Credit Trading Scheme as a qualifying scheme, but the rules for calculating and verifying emissions are still being finalised.

Rediff Money

The Apparel Export Promotion Council says 88% of India's apparel exports come from just 100 districts and has urged a broader export base, wider use of free trade agreements and 100 Champion Districts as export hubs. The target is $100 billion in textile and apparel exports by 2030.

NPPA cuts MRPs on 543 cancer drug brands by up to 93%

Business Standard

India's drug price regulator has revised the maximum retail prices of 543 trade margin-capped cancer drug brands and pack sizes, with cuts of up to 93%, Business Standard reports. Several generics and biosimilars became much cheaper, while some top-selling innovator brands saw no change.

Read more at Business Standard →

Report outlines carbon capture's role in India's net zero plan

A report on India's net zero by 2070 goal describes carbon capture, utilisation and storage as a pathway for industrial emissions that renewables cannot easily remove, such as high-temperature processes. NITI Aayog identifies CCUS as critical for hard-to-abate sectors including steel, cement and oil.
