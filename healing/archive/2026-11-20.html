Too much exercise could leave your brain stuck in overdrive

ScienceDaily · 2026-11-18

Exercising too much without adequate recovery may disrupt brain chemistry in surprising ways. Scientists found that mice subjected to prolonged, intense training had elevated dopamine levels in a brain region linked to memory and emotions, even 48 hours after exercising. The discovery could help explain why overtrained athletes.
