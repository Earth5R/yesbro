IndiGo's Q2 loss narrows to Rs 1,879 crore as fuel costs jump 67%

travel.economictimes.indiatimes.com

InterGlobe Aviation, IndiGo's parent, reported a Q2 FY27 net loss of Rs 1,879.1 crore, narrower than Rs 2,582.1 crore a year ago, as revenue rose 25.9% to Rs 23,363.8 crore. Excluding currency effects, the loss was Rs 723.7 crore against a profit last year.

IndiGo is considering higher fares and a capacity review as aviation fuel costs surge, Storyboard18 reports. CEO Willie Walsh said the airline would have to pass on at least part of the increase, given limited scope for further cost cuts. 'We have no choice,' he said.
